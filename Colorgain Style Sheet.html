Three Rules
For Every Screen

The tests a layout has to pass before it ships.

01Balance

Weigh the two halves of a screen against each other. A big light title on one side earns a short dense paragraph on the other, never two heavy things side by side.

02The Right Word Count

Write to the space. If a paragraph runs past its columns, cut words before moving a margin. Fewer words, set larger, read faster.

03Negative Space

Empty columns are part of the design. They frame the work and tell the eye where to go next. Fill a gap only when the screen is worse without it.

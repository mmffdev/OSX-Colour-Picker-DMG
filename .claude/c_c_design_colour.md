## The eight step colours, in order

Mixed from orange, pale blue and teal, at full strength: Rick asked for true bright, 2026-10-07. One per wizard step, never reused as meaning elsewhere. Every card in the row is at full colour whatever its state; the numeral on it is always ink, so each colour carries ink at 3:1 or better (the self-test checks).

## Where colour goes

Colour is the user's work: swatches, tiles, the step cards, the 10-point band. The interface itself is ink on paper. The one accent is Orange #EC7A3C, for focus rings and the grid overlay only. The product's own colour, Blue Ribbon #2456F5, is in `Brand.swift` and marks the footer's current colour and the halo's wedge; it is not a UI accent.

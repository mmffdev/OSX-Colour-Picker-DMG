# Layout elements

All are Cards on the Paper, hairlines for edges, the arrow for anything that opens.

## Headers

**The app, 64 high.** Four cells: the wordmark (bold lowercase "colorgain", a stand-in until the logo), the tabs, Search as a Soft placeholder, then a mini slider and the avatar. All on one baseline.

**A page.** A Split: the section number in column 1, the two-tone Title from 2 to 7, two captions of metadata in 9 to 11, the arrow in 12. Hairline beneath.

**The wizard.** The 10-point band in the step's colour across the very top, then "STEP 02 OF 08" as a Label on the left and Skip Setup as a quiet button on the right.

## Rails

A Card, 22 top padding, heading row with the arrow, then groups: a Quiet group title, rows of 6/20 with the name left and a tabular count right as caption. The chosen row is Medium with a Mist fill and a dot after its name. The context rail is a table: Quiet column titles on a Rule, rows on Mist hairlines, counts right-aligned, a strip of three swatches before a palette's name. The history rail's rows carry a 22 colour square, name over a caption of what happened, time right.

## Footer, 48 high

Left: a 10 square of the current colour, its name, its hex as caption. Centre: three actions as text, the live one Medium. Right: the mini slider and the release line, "Release v3.0  39ddca5", as tabular caption. The footer is the one place the build hash lives.

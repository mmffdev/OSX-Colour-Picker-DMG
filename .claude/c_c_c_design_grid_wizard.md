# The wizard's frame

Borderless: no title bar, no button bar, a seamless 1040 × 620 panel, radius 10, one shadow. Paper ground, no dark version.

| Part | Place |
|---|---|
| Band | top edge, full width, 10 high, the current step's colour; a step change slides the new colour in the direction of travel and pushes the old one off |
| Top line | y 34, margins 48: "STEP 02 OF 08" Label left, Skip Setup quiet button right, one baseline |
| Title | from y 96, columns 1 to 5, Title style at 44 with the target in front, two-tone |
| Words | columns 7 to 12 from y 96: Lead, then fields each on a hairline Rule with a caption label above and the value at 17 with its quiet action on the same baseline |
| Actions | right margin, y 360: Back quiet, Continue primary, one baseline |
| Steps | bottom 40, margins 48, eight cards in a row, 4 apart, rows 58 label / 66 chip |

A step card: Card ground, the name at 13 in the white top, the numeral at 40 Thin flush right and bottom in the coloured chip. Done cards are solid; the current card carries the barber pole, 10 on 10 off at −45°, moving, and does not rise; cards to come are the colour at 34% with the number at 55%. On first open the cards fly in from the right, 70ms apart, and settle together.

The halo step keeps only the band and the top line; the dial sits dead centre, grown from a target glyph at the end of the page's entrance, with one instruction caption beneath that changes as the user turns the ring.

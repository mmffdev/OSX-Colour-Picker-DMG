# Approved direction
Experience mode: two large miniature islands on a pale blue-grey studio surface. The illustration owns the page; system typography and shared controls recede.
Use true perspective with upright verticals, faceted slate cliffs, irregular yellow-green turf, ivory/coral towers, transparent turquoise water and submerged rocks. Left tower grows vertically; right has a spiral stripe and turns about its vertical axis as it grows. Growth overshoots, compresses, rebounds and settles. Three sparse island-shaped ripples expand and fade; edge waves move continuously. Respect reduced motion.
All UI text is at least 11px. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, stage names and disabled boundary controls are required. Keep all assets local.

Each upward stage starts with a 120ms smoke lead-in at its construction joint. A horizontal ring of overlapping warm-white cloud lobes expands in world space, separates into varied puffs, and evaporates over 1.4 seconds. The smoke stays independent of the tower twist and respects reduced motion and slow motion.

Backward steps release the same smoke ring at the end of the 600ms collapse, at the disappearing section’s construction joint. Changing direction replaces the pending burst; slow motion scales both the collapse delay and smoke lifetime.

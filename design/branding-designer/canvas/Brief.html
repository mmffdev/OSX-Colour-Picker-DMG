02.1The brief
Fernhill asked for a brand that could sit on a hand-written chalkboard without looking like it came from a city agency. Everything in this pack answers that sentence.
Written with Mara Fernhill on 2 October 2026 and agreed on the 6th. The pack is for the family, two staff and the sign-maker.
AgreedW02
“We want people to feel the hill before they read the name.”Mara Fernhill, owner · kitchen table, 2 October

Who they areA third-generation nursery on a south-facing hill. Everything sold is grown on site. Sixty regulars know the family by name; the rest find them through the lane sign and the market stall.

What they needA look the family can apply themselves: a leaflet, the stall banner, plant labels, a one-page website and a van. No agency on retainer, no designer on staff.

What the pack must doGive them three colours they can match at the printer, one typeface they already own, and a short rule for every photograph. Say why each was chosen, so the next decision follows the same logic.

What is outNo logo work in this pack. The wordmark stays as it is until the signage round in spring.

05.2Hierarchy
Six levels, each a job. Sizes grow by a fixed ratio from an 11 pt body, so a leaflet, a sign and a web page share one scale.
Leading is a multiplier of the size: 0.95 for display, 1.5 for body. Tracking tightens as type grows.

Decision
Six levels from one 11 pt base, ratio 1.333.
Why
The client prints leaflets at home and posts on a phone. One scale that survives both means a sign and a web page never argue about size. Six levels is as few as a price list needs.
Alternatives considered
Golden ratio 1.618 · rejected, display ran to 120 pt on A5
Nine levels (Material 3) · rejected, the client has four writers and no designer

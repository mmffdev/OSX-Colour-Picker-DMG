Decision
One green carries the brand. Two lift it.
Why
Customers meet Fernhill in a glasshouse, among real greens. One deep green reads as the plant, not the packaging; the two lighter values are the light coming through the leaves.
Alternatives considered
Forest #2E5C3A · rejected, ΔE 4.1 under FOGRA51

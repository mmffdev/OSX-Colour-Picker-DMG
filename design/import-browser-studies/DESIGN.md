# Ten browser mechanisms

Operate mode. An airy neutral gallery surrounds ten deliberately different component worlds. The focus stage has a fixed-width source rail and a selected-source receipt. Compare mode exposes all ten independently interactive specimens. Body labels stay at one horizontal origin at every depth. Inner and outer scroll gutters always reserve their width. Single-click folders open/select; clicking an open folder closes only its subtree, retaining descendants' expansion state. Native buttons provide keyboard access; file checkboxes support selection. Reduced motion disables transitions.

01 Iris: moving rectangular pole marker broadens into a header. 02 Folio: coral overlapping folder tabs. 03 Spine: deep blue book-spine sections and circular stops. 04 Ledger: mint register with aligned metadata. 05 Ribbon: cobalt bands on yellow paper. 06 Timeline: ochre circular junctions on a continuous line. 07 Colour blocks: lilac fields distinguish levels through colour rather than indentation. 08 Contour: petrol outlines and hollow capsules. 09 Bracket: raspberry bracket endpoints enclose open sections. 10 Fold: graphite paper folds with gold seams.

Fixture folders include Home, Macintosh HD, external media, Trips – Iceland, 2018 – Iceland, Beach and image files. All controls act locally; no external assets or network requests are required.

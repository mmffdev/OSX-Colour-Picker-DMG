Inspector

Select a palette to inspect its name, colour preview and catalogue context. Open Swatches opens its colours. Set Picker Target directs subsequent screen samples into that palette. Collapsing the panel leaves the selection and palette files intact.

Panel Controls

Use the disclosure arrow to collapse or expand the panel. Where available, drag the six-dot handle to move the panel between rails. The outlined question-mark hexagon opens this guide without changing your working selection.

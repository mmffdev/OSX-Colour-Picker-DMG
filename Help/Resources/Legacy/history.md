# History

Every change you make is a step: picking a colour, renaming a palette, adding a pairing, a sync from your other Mac. The History rail lists them so you can go back.

## Open the rail

Press the **History** button in the toolbar, or choose **View ▸ Show History** (⌘⇧Y). The rail opens on the right.

## Go back to a step

Click any step. The library returns to how it was after that step. The later steps stay in the list, greyed, so you can click forward again. Make a new change from there and the greyed steps are cut off.

## Delete a step

Select a step and press Delete, or the bin button. Deleting a step does not change your work; it only removes that point from the list.

## Play back

Press **Play Back** to watch the library walk from the first step to the current one, a step every half second.

## Settings ▸ History

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Keep a history for this library | On or off, for the library you are in. Each library has its own. |
| Kept in | The history lives in `library.history.json` beside the library file. Show in Finder reveals it; Clear History empties it. |
| Write each project's steps into its project file | A step that changes one project is also listed in that project's file, so whoever receives the project sees how it came about. |
| Steps to save | Unlimited, or a number. Once reached, the oldest step is dropped. |

Steps hold only names, colours, order and tags, never images, so even a long history stays small.

Histograms

Histograms show how the selected palette’s values are distributed across the chosen colour model and channels. Choose All Colours or inspect a single colour. Expand opens a larger view. Warnings separates guard-band failures from passing colours; open a finding to inspect its channels. These are value-range findings, not measured print proofs.

Panel Controls

Use the disclosure arrow to collapse or expand the panel. Where available, drag the six-dot handle to move the panel between rails. The outlined question-mark hexagon opens this guide without changing your working selection.

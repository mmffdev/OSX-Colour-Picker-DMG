Image Destination

The To panel belongs to image intake. Choose where reviewed image colours should go before completing the import. Check the destination catalogue, project or palette so the colours are added to the intended library structure.

Panel Controls

Use the disclosure arrow to collapse or expand the panel. Where available, drag the six-dot handle to move the panel between rails. The outlined question-mark hexagon opens this guide without changing your working selection.

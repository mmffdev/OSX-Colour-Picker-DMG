Using Help

For questions or assistance, email support@colorgain.com. The link opens your email app so you can review and send your message. You can also select and copy the address, or choose Help → Copy Support Email.

All of these guides are bundled with this app and work offline. The left index remains available while reading. Palette Page and Halo provide the full palette workflow guides. The contents panel on each page follows your reading position and links directly to sections.

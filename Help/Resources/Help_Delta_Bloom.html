Delta Bloom

Delta Bloom compares palette master colours with predictions for the selected print condition. Choose a print profile, rendering intent and tolerance, then inspect the palette’s petals. A predicted colour difference is not a physical measurement of your press or display. Expand opens the larger analysis view.

Panel Controls

Use the disclosure arrow to collapse or expand the panel. Where available, drag the six-dot handle to move the panel between rails. The outlined question-mark hexagon opens this guide without changing your working selection.

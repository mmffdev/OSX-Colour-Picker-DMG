Shared dimensions

Controls use the app's 13-point reading size and 11-point labels. Standard rows are 32 or 40 points high, with 4, 8, 12 and 16 point spacing. Form dropdowns retain the Rule border and separate chevron compartment; borderless options are studies for actionbars and compact contexts.

Choosing a system

The stars let you mark favourites while this page is open. They do not change Colorgain. The menu content is illustrative, but opening, selecting, outside click and Escape work so the concepts can be judged in use.
